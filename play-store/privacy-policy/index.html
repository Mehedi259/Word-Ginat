Privacy Policy — Last updated: September 29, 2026

🔒 Introduction

Word Giant ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Word Giant (the "App") handles information when you use our service.

Word Giant is a fully offline dictionary application. We believe in minimal data collection and maximum user privacy.

📊 Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information. Word Giant operates entirely offline and does not:

  • Collect personal data (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Collect usage analytics or browsing history
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Share any data with third parties

💾 Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data only on your device (never transmitted to any server):

  • Saved Words: Words you bookmark or save for later reference
  • Learning Progress: Your flashcard progress and learning statistics
  • App Preferences: Settings such as theme, font size, and notification preferences

All this data is stored using Android SharedPreferences and remains entirely on your device. You can clear this data at any time through your device settings.

🔔 Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send daily word-of-the-day reminders (optional, can be disabled in settings)
  • Vibration: For notification haptic feedback
  • Boot Completed: To reschedule notifications after device restart

No internet permission is required as the App works entirely offline.

🗣️ Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine to pronounce words. This feature operates locally on your device and does not send any data over the internet.

👶 Children's Privacy

Word Giant does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect any data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
